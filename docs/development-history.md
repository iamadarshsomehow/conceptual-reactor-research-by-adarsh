# Development history

The final `somuv4` neutronics directory and `thermal` thermal-hydraulics directory were selected after an iterative sequence of earlier reactor-model folders. Earlier directories remain preserved locally as a research record and are intentionally not bulk-published here.

1. Early geometry and transport-model iterations explored the modelling approach.
2. Intermediate revisions generated CAD/mesh exports and isolated transport runs.
3. The final neutronics workflow consolidated baseline, void, moderator-drain, and loss-of-coolant studies.
4. The final thermal-hydraulics workflow used an OpenMC-derived axial power input for channel-level calculations and sensitivity scans.

Future additions should cite reviewed milestones instead of uploading temporary meshes, statepoints, or abandoned scripts.
